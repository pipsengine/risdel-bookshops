# Risdel Books v0.22.0 — Module 21

## Expenses, Petty Cash & Operating Cost Management

Cumulative update on v0.21.0. Adds expense categories, request/approval/payment workflow, evidence references, petty cash funds and custodians, replenishment, petty cash transaction ledger, automatic cash/bank posting, automatic double-entry GL posting, dashboard metrics/alerts, permissions and Google Sheets schema GS-019.

### New sheets
- Finance_ExpenseCategories
- Finance_Expenses
- Finance_ExpensePayments
- Finance_PettyCashFunds
- Finance_PettyCashTransactions
- Finance_PettyCashReplenishments

### Accounting
Paid operating expenses automatically post to the mapped expense account and cash/bank or petty cash control account. Petty Cash account 1010 is seeded.
