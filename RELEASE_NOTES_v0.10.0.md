# Risdel Books v0.10.0 — Module 09 Customers & CRM

Cumulative update of v0.9.0. Expands the existing POS customer master into a full CRM workspace for individuals, parents, students, schools, corporate accounts, resellers, government/NGO and religious organisations.

## Added
- Customer directory, search, KPI summary and full customer profiles.
- Contacts, addresses and parent/student/account relationships.
- Customer notes, CRM activities, next-action tracking and relationship timeline.
- Credit eligibility, limits, payment terms and outstanding exposure visibility.
- Purchase history and account statements using existing POS/invoice/payment records.
- Segmentation and default commercial segments.
- Loyalty tiers, manual point ledger and loyalty dashboard.
- Commercial price groups, tags, source and account-status management.
- Granular CRM permissions and seeded role assignments.
- Google Sheets schema GS-007 and future SQL migration reference.
